# Release contracts

Releases describe usable outcomes and their proof, not just a bundle of commits.

## Alpha 0.2 — Runnable foundation

**Outcome:** a real user can enter through passwordless authentication, complete a minimal profile, and return to a protected empty product shell that visibly conforms to the design system.

**Included:** public shell, auth/profile flow, protected navigation, settings shells, environment boundaries, migration-backed schema, initial row-level security, health check, CI, and design-system specimen.

**Evidence:** formatting, lint, TypeScript, unit, production-build, browser, accessibility, migration, and RLS checks pass. No invitation or private contribution content is accepted yet.

## Alpha 0.3 — Private-pair Exchange

**Outcome:** two independently authenticated people can form one private Space, choose against a shared prompt, and reveal their contributions exactly once after both lock.

## Alpha 0.4 — Complete loop and data

**Outcome:** a pair can experience, reflect, preserve a Chapter, and begin again while content-free lifecycle events describe where the loop succeeds or stalls.

## Alpha 0.5 — Pilot readiness

**Outcome:** recovery, reminders, moderation, export/deletion, accessibility, operational monitoring, and policy controls make a small controlled pilot responsible to run.
